# Spark Shop: Idle Repair Empire

Official store title (30 characters): **Spark Shop: Idle Repair Empire**

Home-screen / short app name: **Spark Shop**

## English

Short description (maximum 80 characters):

Build an idle repair empire, one tiny bench and glowing circuit at a time.

Full description:

A customer walks in. “It worked yesterday!” A tiny repair bench, a soldering iron,
and a few shop coins are all you need to give forgotten electronics another chance.

In Spark Shop: Idle Repair Empire, turn that little bench into a busy repair empire.
Fix fictional radios and tape decks, then grow through five eras of electronics:
living-room classics, pocket gadgets, connected devices and modern power systems.

• Grow 30 repair stations across five eras, from the 1980s to the 2020s.
• Hire a manager for every station. Managed benches keep earning while you are away,
  up to eight hours by default and up to 24 hours with research.
• Spot a glowing faulty component in Fault Hunt. Tap in time for a bonus; missing
  one never costs you progress.
• Reach station milestones, complete daily jobs, collect achievements and invest
  permanent Badges across 40 research ranks.
• Build your Reputation and start fresh with a permanent income advantage.
• Enjoy a seven-day reward cycle and rotating weekend workshop events.

All eras and core progression are reachable without watching an ad or paying.
Optional rewarded videos offer clearly displayed rewards such as double offline
collection or a two-hour income boost. There are no forced interstitials, app-open
ads or gameplay banners. Ad availability depends on your connection and region.

Optional Spark Shop Pro adds permanent ×1.5 income, automatic welcome-back doubling,
automatic check-in boosts and supply collection, with fewer ad prompts. The price
is shown by the store before purchase. Restore purchases from Settings.

Play in English or Turkish, control sounds and haptics, reduce animations and keep
a text backup of your local progress. The shop runs locally; an internet connection
is needed for videos, purchase verification and store transactions.

Small bench. Big ambitions. The next broken gadget is waiting.
