## Game progress on your device

Spark Shop stores progress, settings, tutorial state, a previous-save backup and a
small event log on your device. The event log records offered, started, rewarded
and failed optional ads and their placement names. It retains at most 500 entries;
no Firebase analytics implementation or cloud save is connected in this version.
Game saves do not include your name, email or contacts. An age-band preference
(under 18 / 18 or older) is stored locally to configure advertising privacy; we do
not request your birth date. Android automatic backup is disabled by the app.

Exporting a save creates text that you control. Importing replaces local progress.
Resetting progress clears the game state; uninstalling or clearing app data removes
local saves and the local event log. Store purchase records may remain with the
purchase providers and can restore a valid Pro entitlement.

## Optional advertising

Spark Shop offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob / Google Mobile
Ads SDK. Ads are requested only after the User Messaging Platform (UMP) privacy
process permits requests. Declining an optional video does not prevent play.

The advertising SDK may collect and share device/account identifiers (including
Android advertising ID and app set ID where available), IP-derived approximate
location, app interactions including ad views/clicks, diagnostics and device
performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention
and service operation. Exact data handling depends on SDK version, consent,
regional rules, device settings and any mediation providers you enable. The app
does not request GPS, camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library access.

UMP may present privacy choices where applicable before advertising requests.
Settings → Privacy options reopens the Google privacy form when required. You can
also manage your advertising settings through Android. Under-age flags are applied
for users selecting the under-18 band. A privacy choice does not necessarily stop
all SDK processing needed to deliver permitted contextual/limited ads.

See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Mobile Ads data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## Purchases

The optional Pro product is processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. The
app receives product and entitlement status and does not receive your payment-card
details. These providers may process transaction/purchase history, product IDs,
store/account or app-specific identifiers, entitlement and diagnostic data to
validate purchases, prevent fraud, restore access and handle refunds. RevenueCat
may create an anonymous app-specific customer identifier; no game account is needed.

See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) and the
Google privacy policy above. Provider retention follows their policies and your
publisher configuration: **[CONFIRM PROVIDER RETENTION AND DELETION PROCESS]**.

## Notifications and device permissions

Local reminders are opt in, scheduled on this device and limited to at most one
per day. No remote push token is uploaded by the game. You can turn reminders off
in Settings or Android notification settings. Haptics are optional. Internet and
network-state access support ads and purchases; advertising ID access supports the
ad SDK; Play Billing access supports the Pro product. SDK infrastructure may also
declare wake-lock, boot and foreground-work permissions; the game does not start
an ongoing gameplay foreground service or require exact alarms.
